
# Multiplication: 2-Digit by 1-Digit

**Target Level: Class 5**

## 1\. The Vertical Setup

To multiply vertically, we place the larger number on top and the smaller number (the multiplier) underneath, aligned by their place values.

-----

## 2\. Step-by-Step Method (No Carrying)

Let's solve: **23 × 3**

| Step | Action | Visualization |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Step 1** | Multiply the **Ones**: $3 \times 3 = 9$. Write 9 in the ones column. | $\phantom{1}2\mathbf{3}$ <br> $\times \phantom{1}\mathbf{3}$ <br> ——— <br> $\phantom{12}\mathbf{9}$ |
| **Step 2** | Multiply the **Tens**: $3 \times 2 = 6$. Write 6 in the tens column. | $\phantom{1}\mathbf{2}3$ <br> $\times \phantom{1}\mathbf{3}$ <br> ——— <br> $\phantom{1}\mathbf{6}9$ |

**Final Answer:** 69

-----

## 3\. Step-by-Step Method (With Carrying)

This is where most students get stuck\! Let's solve: **46 × 3**

### Step 1: Multiply the Ones

Multiply $3 \times 6 = 18$.

  * Write the **8** in the Ones column.
  * **Carry over** the **1** to the Tens column (write it small above the 4).

### Step 2: Multiply the Tens

Multiply $3 \times 4 = 12$.

### Step 3: Add the Carry-over

Add the **1** we carried over to the result: $12 + 1 = 13$.

  * Write **13** in the front.

**Final Answer:** 138

-----

## 4\. Scaffold Table for Practice

Use this table to practice the steps. Remember: **Multiply first, then add the carry\!**

| Problem | Step 1 (Ones) | Step 2 (Tens) | Step 3 (Add Carry) | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **14 × 2** | $2 \times 4 = 8$ | $2 \times 1 = 2$ | No carry | **28** |
| **25 × 4** | $4 \times 5 = 20$ (Keep 0, Carry 2) | $4 \times 2 = 8$ | $8 + 2 = 10$ | **100** |
| **38 × 5** | $5 \times 8 = 40$ (Keep 0, Carry 4) | $5 \times 3 = 15$ | $15 + 4 = 19$ | **190** |
| **82 × 6** | $6 \times 2 = 12$ (Keep 2, Carry 1) | $6 \times 8 = 48$ | $48 + 1 = 49$ | **492** |

-----

## 5\. Common Mistake to Avoid

> **The Addition Trap:** Some students add the carry-over *before* multiplying.
>
> **Incorrect:** Add carry (1) to 4 to get 5, then $5 \times 3 = 15$. ❌
> **Correct:** Multiply $3 \times 4 = 12$, then add carry (1) to get 13. ✅
