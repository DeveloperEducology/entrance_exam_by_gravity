# Proportions: A Comprehensive Guide

## 1. What is a Proportion?

A proportion is an equation that shows that two ratios are equal. When two ratios have the same value, they are proportional.

**Example:**
The ratio of circles to triangles in two different sets might be the same.

- Set 1: 1 circle for every 2 triangles $\rightarrow \frac{1}{2}$
- Set 2: 2 circles for every 4 triangles $\rightarrow \frac{2}{4}$

Since $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4}$, these ratios form a proportion.

## 2. How to Tell if Two Ratios Form a Proportion

To determine if two ratios form a proportion, you must compare them. One effective way is to find a common denominator.

**Example:**
Determine if $\frac{4}{9}$ and $\frac{20}{45}$ form a proportion.

Find a common denominator (in this case, 45).

Convert $\frac{4}{9}$ to have a denominator of 45:

$$\frac{4 \cdot 5}{9 \cdot 5} = \frac{20}{45}$$

Since $\frac{20}{45} = \frac{20}{45}$, the ratios form a proportion.

## 3. Solving Proportions

When a proportion contains a variable (an unknown quantity), you can solve for it using several methods.

### Method A: Cross Multiplication

This is the most common method. You multiply the values across the "corners" of the proportion.

**Example:** Solve for $x$ in $\frac{6}{15} = \frac{x}{50}$

Multiply across the corners:

$$6 \cdot 50 = 15x$$

Simplify:

$$300 = 15x$$

Divide both sides by 15:

$$\frac{300}{15} = \frac{15x}{15}$$

$$20 = x$$

### Method B: Inverse Operations

You can also solve by isolating the variable using standard algebraic steps.

**Example:** Solve for $x$ in $\frac{6}{15} = \frac{20}{x}$

Multiply both sides by $x$ to get it out of the denominator:

$$\frac{6x}{15} = 20$$

Multiply both sides by 15:

$$6x = 300$$

Divide both sides by 6:

$$x = 50$$

## 4. Real-World Proportion Problems

Proportions are frequently used to solve everyday problems, such as recipes or scaling.

**Example Problem:**
Kate uses 2 cups of strawberries to make 6 yogurt parfaits. How many cups of strawberries will she need to make 9 yogurt parfaits?

Set up the proportion: $\frac{2}{6} = \frac{x}{9}$

Solve:

$$\frac{2}{6} \cdot 9 = x$$

$$\frac{18}{6} = x$$

$$3 = x$$

**Answer:** Kate will need 3 cups of strawberries.

## 5. Percent Problems

Percent problems can be structured as proportions using the following formula:

$$\frac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} = \frac{\text{percent}}{100}$$

**Example:** What is 5% of 48?

Identify the parts:
- **Part:** $x$ (unknown)
- **Whole:** 48
- **Percent:** 5

Set up the proportion:

$$\frac{x}{48} = \frac{5}{100}$$

Cross multiply:

$$100x = 240$$

Solve for $x$:

$$x = 2.4$$

**Answer:** 5% of 48 is 2.4.
